# Flow regime of Israel's streams, from the measured record

Source: Israel Hydrological Service, daily mean discharge per gauging station,
via data.gov.il dataset `level_discharge`. hydrological years 2000/01 – 2023/24.
A day counts as carrying water at more than 0.001 m3/s.

The class belongs to the gauge, not to the name: a stream can be perennial at one
station and rare at another, and several here are.
